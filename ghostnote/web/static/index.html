    How GhostNote Analyzes and Chooses the Optimal Algorithm

    Human hearing is governed by non-linear psychoacoustic masking principles. GhostNote uses multi-feature spectral and temporal signal processing to choose between two fundamentally distinct steganographic techniques:

    Property Echo Hiding DSSS (Direct Sequence Spread Spectrum)
    Primary Domain Temporal / Quefrency Domain (Micro-delays & Cepstrum) Wideband Frequency Domain (Pseudo-Noise Correlation)
    Psychoacoustic Principle Temporal Masking & Haas Integration (< 2ms) Simultaneous Spectral Masking & Sub-audible SNR
    Best Audio Types Harmonic, tonal audio: piano, acoustic guitar, clean solo vocals, strings. Broadband, energetic, noise-like audio: rock, drums, electronic synths, crowd noise.
    Acoustic Signatures Low Spectral Flatness (< 0.15), high crest factor, continuous harmonic decays. High Spectral Flatness (> 0.25), high spectral entropy, frequent transients.
    Lossy MP3 Robustness Moderate (requires lossless WAV to avoid phase cancellation). High (large processing gain pulls PN sequence above MP3 quantization noise).
    Host Signal Interference Host natural pitch harmonics can overlap delay quefrencies. Cancelled out via Informed Embedding before modulation.

    Cryptographic Security Specification

    GhostNote does not embed raw text. Every payload is protected with government-grade authenticated encryption:

    • PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256: 100,000 iterations with a cryptographically secure 16-byte random salt to protect against dictionary and brute-force attacks.
    • AES-256-GCM: 256-bit Advanced Encryption Standard in Galois/Counter Mode (AEAD), ensuring both confidentiality and tamper detection.
    • Binary Preamble & CRC-32: 32-bit Barker sync pattern for sample-accurate frame alignment and CRC-32 checksum for instant corruption detection.
